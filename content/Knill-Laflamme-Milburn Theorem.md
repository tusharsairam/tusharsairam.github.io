>[!definition] KLM Theorem
>The **Knill-Laflamme-Milburn theorem** says that it's theoretically possible to construct a **universal quantum computer** with just these:
>1. Identical single-photon states (no need for photon pairs)
>2. Linear optical elements (beam splitters, phase shifters etc)
>3. Feedforward measurements (measure after run)[^1]

The significance of the KLM theorem stemmed from the fact that one could make a quantum computer with relatively simple and commonplace tools (contextually)

[^1]: [Shtetl-Optimized: Raymond Laflamme (1960-2025)](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=8949)
