**Post-phenomenology** is the study of ==how technology plays a central role in interacting with the world==

Technology and tools actively shape human perception of the world and action

People to follow: Don Ihde, Peter Paul Verbeek
# Examples of human-technology interactions (Engineering)
- Autonomous vehicles (drivers relying on sensors)
- Scientific instruments (telescopes, particle detectors -> view into the particulate universe)
- SAR imaging on EO satellites

[[theory_of_mediation.pdf|Toward a Theory of Technological Mediation - A Program for Postphenomenological Research]] by Peter Paul Verbeek is a good read
